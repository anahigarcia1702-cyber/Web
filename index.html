FAQ

What are Robux?

Robux are the virtual currency used across Roblox experiences. You can spend them on items, avatar gear, game passes and more.

Where are my Robux?

After purchase, Robux are credited to your balance, usually within a few minutes.

Do Robux expire?

No. Once credited, they don't expire.

How to redeem your gift card?

Go to roblox.com/redeem, sign in, enter the PIN and tap Redeem.

When you buy Robux you receive only a limited, non-refundable, non-transferable, revocable license to use Robux, which has no value in real currency.
